Zebrra Sports

AI sports studio · football and dance shows

What the app does

Zebrra Sports is the publishing app of our AI studio. Our hosts — Gol Donna the zebra and Zlat Kadabra the lion — present football recaps (original AI illustrations made from match facts, no match footage) and dance battles of animated characters. The app runs on the creator's own computer as part of TeO Hub OtakOS.

  • It cuts our own finished episodes into vertical 9:16 clips (up to 10 minutes).
  • After the creator connects a TikTok account (Login Kit), it uploads a chosen clip as a draft to the TikTok inbox (Content Posting API, video.upload).
  • The creator opens the draft in the TikTok app, adds the caption, sets privacy and publishes it.
  • It reads only the account's open ID and display name (user.info.basic) to show which account is connected.
